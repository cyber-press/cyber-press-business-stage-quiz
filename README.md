# Executive Growth Hub Business Stage Finder

## Overview

The **Business Stage Finder** is a professional mobile-responsive quiz form for Executive Growth Hub members. It helps entrepreneurs, aspiring business owners, founders, and growth-minded individuals identify their current business stage and receive a practical next step.

The form is designed for Nigerian and American business audiences and includes external resources for business setup, registration, and growth support.

- `Executive_Growth_Hub_Business_Stage_Finder_Form_v3_Mobile.html`  
  Main standalone HTML form file.

- `Executive_Growth_Hub_Business_Stage_Finder_Form_v3_Mobile.zip`  
  Packaged ZIP version of the form.

- `README_Business_Stage_Finder.md`  
  Setup and usage instructions.

## Business Stages Covered

The quiz identifies one of six business stages:

1. **Idea Stage**  
   The person is still deciding what business to start.

2. **Setup Stage**  
   The person has an idea but needs structure, naming, pricing, registration, branding, or planning.

3. **Customer Acquisition Stage**  
   The business exists, but not enough people know about it yet.

4. **Sales Growth Stage**  
   People know about the business, but the owner needs more paying customers.

5. **Systems Stage**  
   The business is getting sales but needs better organization, tracking, delivery, follow-up, and operations.

6. **Scaling Stage**  
   The business has sales and structure and is ready for growth, hiring, supply expansion, partnerships, or new markets.

## Key Features

- Mobile-first responsive layout.
- Multiple-choice answer selection.
- Progress tracker.
- Automatic business stage scoring.
- Recommended next step based on the result.
- 7-day action focus.
- Copyable result summary.
- Sticky collapsible mobile result footer.
- Reset button that clears the quiz and result.
- External resource links for U.S. and Nigerian business owners.

The form includes links to:

- IRS EIN information for U.S. businesses.
- Nigeria Corporate Affairs Commission.
- SMEDAN registration resource for Nigerian MSMEs.

## Notes

This form is a static HTML file. It does not collect, store, or submit user data to a database. Members can complete the quiz directly in their browser and copy their result manually.
